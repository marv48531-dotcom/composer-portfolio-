## 🎬 Featured Project: *The Assignment*
**Production:** Sainto J Productions (in association with Mirabilis)  
**Genre:** African Supernatural Drama / Psychological Thriller  
**Score Strategy:** Afro-Orchestral, Polyrhythmic Percussion, and Dark Atmospheric Soundscapes  

### Story Synopsis
Deeply rooted in West African folklore around sleep postures and "dream walking," *The Assignment* follows a secretive group of boarding school students who lead dual lives. By night, they enter a shared dream realm to conduct rituals, target classmates, and exert spiritual influence over the school. When a new student stands against the cult, a spiritual battle erupts between light and dark.
